# OpenAula v1.8.1

Gamepad Hall Distance Normalization, Auto-Profile Persistence & Hydration, and 1:1 Slider Precision.

## Gamepad Mode & Hall Distance Normalization (Issue #4)

- **Standard Board Bottom-Out Travel**:
  - Implemented `normalizeDistanceUnits(rawDistance, halfScaleUnits)` in `service/native/gamepad_core.h` and integrated with `service/native/hid_bridge.cpp`.
  - Standard `0.01 mm` boards (`HERO 68`, `HERO 84`) that physically bottom out past `3.40 mm` (`> 340 units`) preserve their true travel without being incorrectly halved.
  - Travel halving is strictly confined to `HERO 68 XS` (`halfScaleUnits = true`), which reports raw Hall distance in `0.005 mm` units (`200 units/mm`).
  - Added `await bridge.syncModel()` before native gamepad initialization in `service/gamepad/gamepadController.ts` to ensure the hardware identity is fully resolved.
  - Added regression tests in `tests/native-gamepad.test.cpp` and ensured standalone MSVC compilation with `NOMINMAX`.

## Auto-Profile Switching Persistence & Hydration (Issue #5)

- **Large Profile Sync Body Limit**:
  - Expanded the maximum request body limit on `POST /profiles/sync` in `service/server/router.ts` from 64 KB to 1 MB, preventing `Preset too large` errors when synchronizing multiple profiles with comprehensive layer drafts.
- **Client-Side State Persistence**:
  - Wired `onboardMetadata`, `linkedProfiles`, `inactiveProfiles`, and auto-profile toggle flags (`autoProfileEnabled`, `revertToDefaultOnFocusLoss`, `notifyOnProfileSwitch`) into `useDeviceSyncController.ts` for automatic persistence to `localStorage`.
- **Background Service Profile Hydration**:
  - Introduced `resolveHydratedProfilesFromService` in `src/state/profileHydration.ts` and integrated into `useProfileWorkspace.ts`.
  - When opening a fresh browser session or after clearing browser storage, auto-switch rules and linked profiles stored in the background service `profiles.json` are safely hydrated into the UI rather than being overwritten with blank defaults.
  - Added full test suite in `tests/auto-profile-persistence.test.mjs`.

## 1:1 Slider Precision & Direct Track Mapping

- **Direct Track Coordinate Mapping**:
  - Simplified slider pointer dragging in `src/app/components/sliderDrag.ts` and `src/app/components/useSliderPointerDrag.ts` to map directly 1:1 with pointer coordinates along the track.
  - Added discrete `dragStep` snapping for `RangeControl.tsx` and `VerticalRangeControl.tsx` (e.g. snapping to `0.05 mm` steps on actuation and `0.01 mm` steps on Rapid Trigger sliders).
