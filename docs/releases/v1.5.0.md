# OpenAula v1.5.0

WIN 68 HE MAX Flash Flush 0x23 Auto-Recovery, 200 Hz Hall Stream & Research Synthesis.

## Auto-Recovery from `0x23` Read Timeout During Flash Flush

- Resolved `WIN 68 HE MAX read timed out (opcode=0x23)`:
  - Traced root cause to MCU interrupt masking during SPI/NOR Flash sector erasures (`VA 0x07E28` → `0x11914` → `0x09AF0`). During deferred 500–670 ms Flash flush, the MCU drops incoming vendor packets and subsequently issues an unsolicited `5C 04 FF <ck> | 0A FF <hi> <lo>` completion frame.
  - Implemented automatic retry with backoff for `0x23` readback requests in `XsydDeviceManager`, transparently recovering when an erase occurs mid-request.

## 200 Hz High-Speed XSYD Hall Sensor Streaming

- Optimized Hall telemetry for ultra-low latency:
  - Streamlined polling in `src/protocol/hero68/hallStream.ts`, `service/hall/hallController.ts`, and native `hid_bridge.cpp`.
  - Replaced redundant pressed-state requests with a single initial `0x12 0x03` seed, executing steady-state polling in 1 round trip with 3:1 prioritized sampling for depressed keys over idle keys.
  - Reached ~200 Hz stream cadence on WIN 68 HE MAX.
